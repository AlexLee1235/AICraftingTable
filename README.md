**AI Crafting Table** adds a crafting table powered by AI. Instead of fixed recipes, it creates **brand new items and blocks** based on how you arrange materials in the grid.

***

## Feature

*   🧠 **AI-Powered Crafting** – Place any combination of items and let the AI generate a unique result.
*   🎨 **Custom Items** – Every result gets its own name, texture and use: tools, weapons, food and more.
*   🧱 **Custom Blocks (New in 2.0)** – The AI can now create blocks too, ready to place and build with.
*   🧪 **Shape Matters** – The layout of ingredients changes the result, so try different patterns!
*   🗃️ **Saved Results** – Everything you make stays in your world and can be crafted again from the recipe book.

***

## Usage

*   Craft the AI Crafting Table: a crafting table surrounded by four diamonds.
*   Place your ingredients in it and click the arrow button.
*   Pick one of the three ideas, and your new item or block is created!
*   If the texture is broken or looks bad, you can click the re-generate button near the result slot to regenerate the item.
*   You can remove item with command /aicraft removeItem id

***
